Three moves, endless shop

The whole game fits in your thumb

No timers screaming at you. No energy bars. Just three motions, layered into hundreds of hand-built levels.

🔥

Melt

Hold to heat the pan. Pull off too early and it's grainy — too late and it burns. The window is short, and that's the point.

🌀

Swirl

Drag to fold color through the syrup. Every swirl pattern is yours — no two candies in your shop ever look quite the same.

🍬

Serve

Plate your batch for the day's customers. Nail their order and they'll come back tomorrow with a bigger one.
